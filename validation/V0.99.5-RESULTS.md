## Open physical release blockers

- **CONFIRMED by user:** V0.99.4 still hits `DMA DEADLINE / AUDIO STOP` when usersamples retrigger quickly, requiring a restart. **V0.99.5 has NOT been physically tested, so it must not be called fixed or glitch-free.**
- Exact ISR/DMA wall-clock budget and SPI NOR IRQ-mask latency on a real FM1 remain unknown. Streaming tails continue to require foreground flash reads.
- Six-lane mixed long samples plus USB stems, LCD redraw and real MIDI retriggers require a long duration hardware stress test.
- Do not call this V1.0 RC; flash only with verified independent recovery. Existing HOME+PLAY (hold 1 s) is a possible software audio rearm for deadline stops, not a fix for the underlying cause.
