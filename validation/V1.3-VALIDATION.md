## Limits of these checks

The developer reports extensive BEETLE BLUE testing on **two physical M-VAVE FM1 units**. We do not have an independently documented install / stock V15 rollback test of this **exact V1.3 binary**, nor a hardware instrument attached to this environment. Package verification is not a substitute for on-device OTA and recovery testing. Do not assume stock V16 or arbitrary firmware is permitted by the stock V15 whitelist.
