## Limits

- Software host audio renders are **not FM1 DMA or real audio-output measurements**.
- Prior hardware-reported double-kick `DMA DEADLINE / AUDIO STOP` has not been proved resolved.
- Long-run FM1 audio scheduling, analogue levels, real USB audio, NOR/storage migration, global BBK1 backup, and OTA/recovery remain unverified.
- MONOLITH reverse is reverse-style synthesis rather than literal PCM reverse. Storage V2 user sample streams lack literal reverse/long delay and full FX history.
