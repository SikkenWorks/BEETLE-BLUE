## Memory savings
V0.93 included a 50,800-byte 8kHz IMA ADPCM bank. V0.94 no longer includes that bank; it instead stores very small ROM coefficients and sine table. App headroom grew from about 8.7 KB (V0.93) to 58,688 bytes. Digital 44.1kHz synthesis provides bandwidth beyond the previous 8kHz files but is NOT an objective guarantee of better perceived sound than those originals.
